02 OUR IDEA

Our idea: SafeShell

Installed once, active in every terminal you open.

Protect

Blocks harmful commands and explains why.

Prove

Runs the command in a sandbox first and shows the real impact.

Translate

Understands plain English. No need to remember commands.

Undo

Snapshots let you roll back if something still goes wrong.

You always have the final say: SafeShell warns, it never locks you out.
