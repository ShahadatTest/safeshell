# Code of Conduct

SafeShell is a small project run by students and open to everyone. We want it to be a place
where people can ask questions, report bugs and send improvements without being made to feel
unwelcome. This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and explain things without talking down.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Critique code and designs, never the person.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Reviews exist to improve the project.
- Say when you do not know something, and credit other people's work.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information (including addresses, emails, or keys) without permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated off-topic spam
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Security reports are not conduct reports

Found a vulnerability? Do not post it in a public issue. Use GitHub's private
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on the repository (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#reporting-security-problems)).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, hide
or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private report option on the repository, or contact a maintainer
listed in the [README](README.md#team) directly. Reports are handled privately, and reporters are
protected from retaliation.

Consequences, in order: a private warning; a public warning; a temporary ban from the project;
a permanent ban. Maintainers will use the least severe step that stops the behaviour.
